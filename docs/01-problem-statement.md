# Problem Statement

## Project
Semester Budget Planner: a mobile-first app for college students.

## The Problem
Many students receive a fixed amount of money for the semester (allowance,
stipend, or savings) and have no simple way to pace it. They spend freely
early on, lose track of small daily expenses, and run out of money weeks
before the semester ends. Existing finance apps are built for salaried adults
and are too complex for a student's needs.

## Target Users
- College students aged 18-23, often living in hostels or PGs
- Limited, fixed income with no monthly salary
- Spend mostly through UPI and cash, and use their phone daily

## Proposed Solution
The app:
1. Breaks the semester budget into weekly budgets
2. Alerts the user before they overspend
3. Shows where their money actually goes (category breakdown)

## Goals
- Set up a semester budget in under 2 minutes
- Log an expense in 3 taps or fewer
- Make the user aware of overspending before it happens, not after

## Constraints
- Mobile-first design
- Wireframe-level deliverable (low-fidelity, Figma)
