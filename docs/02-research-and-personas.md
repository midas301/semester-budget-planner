# User Persona

## Persona: Aarav, 20
**Role:** 2nd-year engineering student, lives in a hostel
**Money situation:** Gets ₹30,000 from parents at the start of the semester
(about 5 months), plus occasional pocket money.

### Behaviour
- Pays almost everything by UPI (food, cabs, subscriptions, outings)
- Never writes expenses down, only checks the bank balance
- Spends more in the first month and panics near the end

### Goals
- Make the money last the whole semester
- Know how much he can spend this week without stress
- Spot where the money is leaking (food delivery, cabs, etc.)

### Pain Points
- "I don't realise I've overspent until the balance is nearly zero."
- Finance apps feel complicated and built for working adults.
- Tracking every expense manually feels like a chore.

### Needs from the app
- Simple setup, fast expense entry
- A clear "left this week" number
- Early warnings, not just after-the-fact reports
