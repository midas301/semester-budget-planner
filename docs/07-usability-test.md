# Student Usability Test

## Status

**Preparation complete; sessions and findings not yet recorded.** Recruit 3–5 students who manage a semester allowance, stipend, savings, or similar fixed pool of money. Do not fill in results before running sessions.

## Purpose

Check whether students can create a weekly plan, record and correct a purchase, understand the over-budget state, and find their spending history. Observe what they do before explaining the interface.

## Session format (10–15 minutes)

1. Ask consent to take notes; explain that the product is being tested, not the participant.
2. Ask: “How do you usually keep track of money during a semester?” Do not ask for a precise personal balance.
3. Give the participant the tasks below, one at a time. Read only the task prompt; do not demonstrate the steps.
4. Note completion, time, backtracks, help requests, and the participant's own words.
5. Ask the closing questions and thank them. Do not collect names or financial information in the report.

## Task prompts

| # | Say to the participant | Success looks like |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | “You have ₹12,000 to last from the start of this month to the end of the next three months. Set up a weekly plan and split it across categories that make sense to you.” | Saves dates and total; understands the weekly estimate; completes category allocation. |
| 2 | “You spent ₹120 on lunch today. Add it to your plan.” | Saves the right amount, date, and a reasonable category without moderator help. |
| 3 | “That entry should have been ₹150 and the category should be Food. Correct it.” | Finds and edits the entry. |
| 4 | “Find the purchase you just added and remove it.” | Finds delete, understands the confirmation, removes the entry. |
| 5 | “You have spent more than this week's plan. What does the app want you to know, and what could you do next?” | Correctly interprets the overspend and identifies a next action without feeling blamed. |
| 6 | “Check how much went to each category this month.” | Finds and interprets the category breakdown in Insights. |

If a usable build is not available, run tasks 1, 2, 5, and 6 with the Figma prototype and mark edit/delete as **not tested**. Do not count a static frame as evidence of working behavior.

## Closing questions

- “What felt easiest?”
- “Where did you hesitate or feel unsure?”
- “Was any wording confusing or judgmental?”
- “What would you expect to happen after saving or deleting a spend?”
- “What is one change that would make this useful during a busy week?”
