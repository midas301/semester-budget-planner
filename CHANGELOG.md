# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-13)
### Added
- Semester plan: dates, total budget, automatic weekly budgets
- Weekly category allocation (Food, Transport, Books, custom)
- Expense logging with category, date and note; edit and delete
- Overspend warning at 80% and alert at 100% of the weekly budget
- Weekly review with category breakdown and transaction history
- Insights: monthly spending chart and semester projection
- Local-only storage on the device (no account, no bank connection)
- Android build via Capacitor
